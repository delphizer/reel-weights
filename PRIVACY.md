# Privacy Policy for Reel Weights

_Last updated: October 1, 2026_

Reel Weights is a browser extension that merges and ranks movie and TV searches from IMDb. This policy explains what it does with your information.

## The short version

Reel Weights does not collect, store, sell, or share your personal data. It has no servers, no accounts, no analytics, and no tracking. Everything it saves stays in your browser.

## What it accesses

To show your results, Reel Weights requests IMDb search pages and sub-genre data from IMDb, using the IMDb session already signed in to your browser. Those requests go directly from your browser to IMDb; they never pass through anyone else.

If you're signed in, IMDb uses your account to apply filters such as "hide titles I've watched." Reel Weights never sees or reads your IMDb password, cookies, or account details. Your browser attaches your session to the requests, the same way it does when you visit imdb.com.

## What it stores

Reel Weights saves the following in your browser's local extension storage:

- your settings: sliders, filters, searches, and display options
- titles you chose to hide
- the most recent search results and cached sub-genre tags

This data never leaves your device. Uninstalling the extension deletes it.

## Third parties

The only site Reel Weights communicates with is IMDb (www.imdb.com and api.graphql.imdb.com). IMDb's handling of your requests is covered by IMDb's own privacy policy. Reel Weights is not affiliated with IMDb.
